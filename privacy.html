YOUR DATA

Privacy & local storage

This prototype saves task titles, descriptions, projects, priorities, dates, and statuses in your browser’s local storage. It does not send task data to a database and includes no analytics or advertising scripts.

Who can see my tasks?

Anyone with access to this browser profile may be able to view them. This demo has no authentication. Do not store passwords, personal records, or confidential work here.

How do I delete my data?

Use Reset demo on the task board to replace your tasks with sample tasks, or clear this site’s storage in your browser settings to remove it entirely. Data does not sync between devices and may be lost if browser storage is cleared.

Hosting

Once deployed, the hosting provider may process request metadata such as IP addresses and browser information to serve the website. Connected accounts and database features will require an updated privacy notice before release.
