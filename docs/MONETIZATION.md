# Monetization without interrupting work

**Current status:** Orbit contains no advertising code, publisher IDs, or analytics scripts. Deployment, AdSense site review, and account details are still pending.

## Which Google product?

AdSense lets publishers earn from ads on their content. Google Ads is the advertiser product used to pay for campaigns. A polished interface or SEO metadata alone does not establish AdSense eligibility: Google asks for original, useful content and policy compliance. See [AdSense eligibility](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9724/eligibility-requirements-for-adsense?hl=en-GB).

## Proposed placement

Keep task boards, account screens, task forms, project dashboards, and focus sessions ad-free. These screens exist to help someone finish work.

After deployment and site approval, consider a single responsive ad within a substantial public planning guide, after its useful content. Examples of worthwhile guides: breaking a deadline into steps, planning a small website launch, or managing semester assignments. Write them for real readers, with practical examples; do not publish thin pages merely to create ad inventory.

Google excludes ads on screens with absent or low-value publisher content and certain behavioral screens. A landing page is not automatically eligible just because it has text. This placement plan is a product recommendation, not a promise of approval. See [publisher-content policy](https://support.google.com/publisherpolicies/answer/11112688?hl=en).

## Integration requirements

Before adding a live unit:

1. Publish the production site and complete Google's account/site review requirements.
2. Supply the actual publisher and ad-slot IDs. Do not invent or copy someone else's IDs.
3. Confirm the selected public page qualifies for ads and update privacy/consent behavior for the chosen ad configuration and audience.
4. Label the placement **Advertisements** or **Sponsored links**. Keep it visually separate from navigation and action buttons.
5. Reserve responsive space so an arriving ad does not shift the reading position.
6. Review the current Content Security Policy and allow only services the integration needs; do not simply disable it.
7. Test on mobile, with blocked scripts, slow networks, and an empty ad response. The page must remain useful.

Avoid overlays, interstitial interruptions, or ads beside Save/Delete controls. Google advises clear separation and a clean reading experience. See [ad placement guidance](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/1282097?hl=en).

## Evaluating results

Measure returning users and page usefulness before optimizing revenue. Approval, traffic, ad availability, and earnings are not guaranteed. Website ranking also needs live indexing and performance verification; the current SEO implementation is a technical foundation.

Do not send task titles, checklist contents, project labels, or account details to advertising services. Revisit this plan when an approved production site is available.
