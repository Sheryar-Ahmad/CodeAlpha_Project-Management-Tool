A SMALL TOOL FOR MEANINGFUL WORK

Everything in its place.

▦

See the whole picture

Give each project a brief and milestones. See tasks on a board or list, then compare recorded project dates on a timeline.

◷

Keep deadlines visible

Review the month on your calendar, plan a task for any day, and use Today or Upcoming for the deadlines closest to you.

↗

Find your next step

Open the Action Center for blocked, overdue, and urgent work. See why each task needs attention, then choose a clear next step.

✓

Keep useful context close

Keep notes, decisions, and meeting outcomes in a project notebook. Turn a meeting into an editable follow-up task.

↻

Skip the repeated setup

Start from a checklist template, duplicate useful work, or repeat a daily, weekly, or monthly task while keeping completed history.

◷

Give your effort a record

Focus with a quiet timer. Separately record time you worked and review weekly totals by day or project.
