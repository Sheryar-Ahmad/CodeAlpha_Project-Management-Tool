YOUR DATA

Privacy & your workspace

Orbit is a project management application by Sheheryar Ahmad. This notice describes the current personal-workspace implementation.

Local demo

Demo tasks are saved in this browser’s local storage. They are not uploaded to the account database. Anyone using the same browser profile may see them. Use Reset demo to replace them with samples, or clear the site’s browser storage to remove them completely.

Accounts and tasks

Account mode stores your name, email address, a salted password hash, and your tasks in MongoDB. Plaintext passwords are not stored. Task data includes titles, project labels, descriptions, priorities, statuses, blocker reasons, due dates, checklist steps and their completion states, notes, resource link labels/URLs, archive/trash state, repeat schedule and occurrence links, and timestamps.

Focus timer

Timer settings, remaining time, and a running session’s deadline are saved in this browser’s local storage. Demo and account timers use separate keys. They are not synced to MongoDB or other devices. Your written focus goal is not stored. Anyone with access to this browser profile may inspect its local storage; clear site storage to remove timer records.

Sessions and request limits

A required HTTP-only cookie keeps you signed in for up to seven days. The database stores a digest of the session token, its account reference, and its expiry. Signing out revokes the current session. Request limits use hashed client-address counters with expiry dates to reduce abuse.

Task exports

Export tasks downloads your task content, including archived and trashed records, as a JSON file. The file excludes passwords and session tokens, but your written content may be sensitive. Keep it private. Exporting does not remove the stored records.

Deleting data

Archiving keeps a task saved outside active work. Delete moves a task to Trash, where you can restore it to the board or permanently delete it. Trash is not automatically emptied. Self-service account deletion and password recovery are not implemented yet. Clearing browser data does not delete account records from MongoDB. Do not use this prototype for confidential or regulated information.

Hosting and external services

A deployed instance uses its hosting provider and MongoDB deployment to serve and store data. Providers may process network metadata such as IP addresses. Orbit includes no advertising or analytics scripts and does not intentionally publish private task content.

Development status

Production retention rules, account deletion, recovery, and a support contact must be finalized before a broad public release. The public source repository contains application code, not your account records or database credentials.
